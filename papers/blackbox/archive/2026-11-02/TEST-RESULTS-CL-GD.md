### CL-013 — BLOCKED: no same-day appointment can be created, and no check-in control is rendered
- **What happened.** The request was created and confirmed successfully, but the confirmation could not be dated 2026-11-01. For every clinician in "Assigned clinician", "Available times" rendered empty with the hint *"Choose staff and a date. If no times are available, ask your department administrator to configure the schedule or choose another date."* The same clinician with date 2026-11-02 produced 36 bookable slots, so the slot list is date-driven and 2026-11-01 (Sunday) has no provider availability.
- **Exact messages seen.** `"Appointment time must not be in the past."` (raised when the preferred time 09:00 was submitted on the request form — the request had to be moved to 04:00 PM); the "Available times" hint above; on the confirmed Nov-2 row the only action rendered is a red `aria-label="Cancel appointment"` button.
- **Impact on the run.** The front-desk check-in half of CL-013 could not be executed, and the same wall blocked the guidance appointment check-in in GD-008. CL-005…CL-010 were driven from a queue ticket created through the New Walk-in path (CL-011) instead of a checked-in appointment; all of those cases passed on that ticket.
- **Not verified.** Whether check-in would create a queue entry for a same-day appointment — the app never offered a same-day appointment to check in.
